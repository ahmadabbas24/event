FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Why choose an event management company?

One team handles venue, décor, catering, sound and lighting, so you deal with one person instead of ten vendors, and the day runs on a fixed timeline.

How early should we book?

For weddings and concerts, two to three months ahead gives the best choice of venues and vendors. Birthdays and smaller parties can often be arranged in one to two weeks.

Can we book only one service?

Yes. You can book décor, catering, venue selection or sound and lights on their own, or the complete event.

How do you price an event?

We send an itemised quote based on guest count, venue and the services you choose. Nothing is booked until you approve it.

About IBS

We plan the details so you can enjoy the moment.

IBS Event Management started with a simple idea: the people celebrating should not be the ones running around on the day.

We plan weddings, family functions, concerts and corporate events of every size, from a dholki at home to a walima for a thousand guests. Our team handles design, vendors, logistics and the timeline, and stays on site until the last guest leaves.

We work with decorators, caterers, florists, sound crews and photographers we trust, and we know which ones fit which budget.
